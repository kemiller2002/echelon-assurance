and the owner records, and read-only instructions. None saw another reviewer's

**Structural change.** Rounds 3–5 each found another field whose change was
not reviewed. Rather than adding one rule per field, the governed record
(`src/Echelon.Assurance.Registers/Governed.fs`) digests the governed fields
of every register into a committed snapshot
(`tests/Echelon.Assurance.Tests/governed-record.csv`, 1,909 records; whole rows from round 6). A
change to any governed field fails the tests until the snapshot is
regenerated with `assurance governed`, so every such change appears as a
fixture diff for review. This does not judge whether a change is right; it
guarantees that it is seen. The rule-based checks remain for states that
are false on their face.
