# Multiple-agent review A–F (2026-11-26)

## Provenance

Six reviewers were started as separate Claude Code general-purpose subagents.
Each had its own freshly initialized context, a written brief naming its scope
and the owner records, and read-only instructions. None saw another reviewer's
output or the coordinator's conclusions. The coordinating session reconciled
the findings on evidence, re-reading the cited lines, not by majority. This is
**agent review** (`agent-review`): internal development review under owner
direction DF-ASR-2026-0D0F. It is not organizational independence, a
qualified professional review or an attestation. Agent consensus is not proof.
