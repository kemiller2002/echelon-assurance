## Decision
At the current research and development stage, do not engage legal counsel or purchase recurring independent review. Multiple-agent review, supported by primary-source research and objective deterministic verification where applicable, is sufficient for internal development review. External professional services are not the default means of resolving uncertainty.

## Required review process
Use separately prepared initial analyses, adversarial challenge, alternative interpretations and evidence-based reconciliation. Verify citations and applicable dates; use deterministic tests and traceability checks for executable claims. Record disagreements, assumptions, limitations, unresolved risks and review provenance. Agent consensus alone is not proof. Technical review is not organizational independence, legal advice or external attestation.

- ODP-03: design architectural safeguards and document concentration risk; no additional custodian appointment now. Actual production risk acceptance remains separate.
- ODP-05: continue requirements and development with internal regulatory research; retain sponsor-controlled funds-flow safeguards and activation gates for unresolved material legal risks.

## Boundaries
This decision does not waive legal, contractual or regulatory obligations, authorize production activation with unresolved material legal risks, or claim that agents replace qualified legal advice or external attestation. Reassess external services when legally required, commercially necessary or material uncertainty cannot otherwise be resolved. No expenditure or other repository changes authorized.
