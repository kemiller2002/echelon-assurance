# Charter

## Mission

Establish an AI-native assurance operating model for Echelon Foundry that
minimizes recurring compliance cost and manual administration, generates
evidence as a byproduct of operations, enforces policy technically where
possible, validates control effectiveness independently, reduces dependence on
commercial compliance platforms, supports credible external attestation,
scales across Echelon products, and meets the expectations of financial
institutions and enterprise customers.

The program challenges conventional compliance workflows rather than
automating them unexamined.

## Scope (owner direction, 2026-11-25)

- Organizational assurance, not exclusively SOC compliance. SOC 1 and SOC 2 are
  initial priorities.
- Research, requirements, policy development, architecture specifications and
  validation methodology. **No new software platform** is built in this phase.
- Existing Echelon systems are evaluated before any new software is proposed.
- Unbound is the reference implementation; design is organization-wide.
- Other repositories are read-only; proposed changes are recorded here.
- Repository must remain private; sensitive material is never committed.

## Deliverables

Listed in `README.md`. Completion criteria: charter Section 22 — successive
review passes until additional research yields diminishing returns, with every
material question resolved or recorded in `registers/open-questions.csv`, and a
prioritized roadmap in `roadmap/`.

## Non-goals

- Claiming compliance, certification, attestation or control effectiveness.
- Replacing professional judgment of CPAs, counsel, QSAs or bank partners.
- Reproducing every feature of commercial compliance platforms.
