# POL-02 Code of Conduct and Acceptable Use

## Purpose
Set the conduct expected of people with access to Echelon systems and data, and make it acknowledgeable.

## 01 Ethics and conflicts of interest
1. Personnel act with integrity, comply with law and report suspected misconduct without retaliation.
2. Personnel disclose conflicts of interest, including relationships with charities, donors, sponsors, vendors and financial institutions. Disclosed conflicts are recorded; a conflicted person may not approve a decision affecting the conflict.
3. Gifts or benefits from customers, charities or vendors above a nominal value are disclosed.

## 02 Acceptable use of systems and data
1. Access is used only for assigned work. Data is accessed only when the task requires it.
2. Company data is processed only on managed devices and approved services.
3. Prohibited: circumventing controls, sharing credentials, storing secrets outside the approved vault, exporting restricted data without authorization, testing security of systems without authorization.
4. Personal use of company devices is permitted only where it does not affect security.

## 03 Personnel use of AI tools
1. Personnel may use only AI tools and providers listed as approved for the data class involved (POL-05.01, POL-10.02).
2. Restricted data (customer financial records, personal data, credentials, security findings, evidence) may not be entered into an AI tool unless the tool is approved for that class.
3. AI output used in work products is reviewed by the person who submits it; the person remains accountable.

## Acknowledgement
Personnel acknowledge this policy at onboarding and on each material change. The acknowledgement is recorded as an attributable event (evidence type: acknowledgement record). Re-acknowledgement is required on material change, not merely annually (annual re-signing is `industry-convention`, not a requirement we found).

## Enforcement
Violations are handled through the personnel process, up to termination of access and engagement.
