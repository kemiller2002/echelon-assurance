# POL-13 Customer Operations Policy

## 01 Onboarding and termination
1. Tenant provisioning is automated from an approved order; the customer's authorized administrators and authority matrix are established before production use.
2. On termination, data is returned in a documented format and then deleted or crypto-shredded per contract; deletion is confirmed to the customer with evidence.

## 02 Configuration governance
1. Tenant-specific policies (fee schedules, approval thresholds, grant policies, eligibility rules) are versioned configuration. Changes require approval by the customer's authorized administrator, are effective-dated, and are reported back to the customer.
2. Echelon staff cannot change a tenant's financial configuration without the customer's recorded authorization, except under incident response with notification.

## 03 Complaints
Complaints are recorded with category, received date, owner and resolution; trends are reviewed in management review. Bank partner contracts may impose specific handling and reporting; those are recorded as requirements.

## 04 Customer notification and communications
1. Customers are informed of material system changes, security commitments, incidents affecting them (POL-08), and changes to subprocessors.
2. Customer-facing claims about security or compliance are reconciled to the control catalog and issued reports before publication (POL-01.03).

## 05 Service commitments
Service commitments (availability, support, data handling) are recorded once as requirements and are the source for both contracts and the system description used in any SOC report.
