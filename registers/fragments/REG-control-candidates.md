# Control candidates (REG)

Proposals only; the coordinator assigns `CTL-` ids. Status of every candidate:
`Proposed`. Requirement ids refer to `regulatory/fragments/REG.csv`.
Automation levels L0 (manual) … L5 (autonomous with self-evidence); targets
are recommendations, not observations.

| # | Candidate | Objective | Risk | Mechanism | Target level (reasoning) | Failure mode | Evidence | Independence | Drivers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REG-C01 | Regulatory applicability register with re-classification triggers | Keep each law/framework's applicability current and tied to facts (operating model, contracts, volumes, geographies) | Silent drift into a legal duty (e.g., first EU donor, 2M ACH entries, card intake, bank contract) | Machine-readable trigger rules evaluated against configuration (enabled rails, states with residents, volumes, contract registry); trigger opens a counsel/owner task | L3 (facts are machine-observable; legal judgment stays human) | detective-only (cannot block business change it doesn't see), plus fail-closed hook: production enablement of a rail/geography requires current applicability row | Trigger evaluation log; counsel sign-off records | Counsel review annually; internal agents not independent | REQ-GLBA-001, REQ-STATE-002, REQ-GDPR-001, REQ-NACHA-002, REQ-PCI-001 |
